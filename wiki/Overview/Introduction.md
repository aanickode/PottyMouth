# Introduction

PottyMouth is an iOS application designed to help users limit their usage of certain words or phrases they want to avoid, commonly referred to as "no-no" words. The app records audio through the device's microphone and tracks the frequency of these specified words, providing users with analytics and a "profanity score" based on their usage.

## Overview

The primary goal of PottyMouth is to promote better speaking habits and accountability by allowing users to set their own list of words or phrases they want to limit. The app can be used in various contexts, such as households, classrooms, or social gatherings, to encourage more mindful language usage. Sources: [README.md:1-18]()

## Features

### Word Tracking

Users can input a list of words or phrases they want to avoid using. The app then records audio through the device's microphone and tracks the frequency of these specified words or phrases. Sources: [README.md:8-11]()

### Profanity Score

PottyMouth calculates a "profanity score" based on the total number of times the user has said any of the listed words or phrases. This score serves as a quantitative measure of the user's progress in limiting their usage of these words. Sources: [README.md:9-10]()

### Analytics

The app provides users with analytics and statistics on the frequency of each listed word or phrase they have used. This allows users to identify their most commonly used "no-no" words and work on improving their language habits accordingly. Sources: [README.md:11-12]()

### Word Management

Users can add or remove words or phrases from their list in real-time, allowing them to adapt the app's tracking to their changing needs. Sources: [README.md:12-13]()

## Use Cases

PottyMouth can be utilized in various scenarios, including:

1. **Households**: Parents can use the app to encourage their children to avoid using certain words or phrases, promoting better language habits within the family. Sources: [README.md:14-15]()

2. **Educational Contexts**: Students and teachers can use the app to improve their speaking habits and adhere to important classroom policies by monitoring their language usage through the app's analytics. Sources: [README.md:15-16]()

3. **Social Gatherings**: The app can be used in party settings or other social events to encourage mindful language usage and potentially add an element of friendly competition or accountability among participants. Sources: [README.md:17]()

## Data Storage

PottyMouth uses the MongoDB Realm database to store user information, such as their list of words or phrases to track. However, the app strictly does not store the user's audio transcript or any recorded audio data to maintain privacy. Sources: [README.md:19-20]()
